<objective>
Convert the 15 legacy `.mdx` files in the blog and case-studies collections to `.md`, proving by
byte-for-byte comparison of the rendered HTML that this is a pure format change with no content
loss.

setup. These 15 files currently build only incidentally; any bump to `@astrojs/mdx` or change to the
content loader could break the entire case-studies section and nine blog posts without warning.
Requirements TECHDEBT-01 and TECHDEBT-02.

Output: 9 blog posts and 6 case studies renamed from `.mdx` to `.md`, with identical file bodies and
identical rendered output.
</objective>

  untouched. Do not run `npm run format` (Prettier) against `src/content/`.

  randomly generated Preline accordion id that changes on every build, so they are not byte-stable
  even between two consecutive builds of unchanged source. Route-inventory parity and sitemap parity
  still cover them.
- The two `case-study-5` / `case-study-6` stubs are 51-byte frontmatter-only placeholders carrying
  `draft: true` and no body. Migrate their extension for format consistency and change nothing else.
