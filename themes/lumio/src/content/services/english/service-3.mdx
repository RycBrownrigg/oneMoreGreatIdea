## What You Get [.text-h4]

A technical due diligence report that an investment committee can actually use — not a checkbox exercise, but a substantive evaluation of what the target company's technology really is, what risks it carries, and whether the team can execute.

This surfaces the issues a pitch deck doesn't mention: the undocumented vendor dependency that makes the platform unportable, the founding team's actual depth versus their claimed expertise, the architecture decisions that will require a painful rewrite at Series B scale.

## Specialization [.text-h4]

This engagement is best suited for targets in:

- **Streaming, OTT, and media technology** — platform architecture, DRM, CDN, encoding infrastructure, and content delivery at scale
- **Web3 and blockchain infrastructure** — protocol design, smart contract security, tokenomics, cross-chain systems, and DeFi architecture
- **Telecom and connected media** — multi-device delivery, subscriber management, content licensing, and B2B2C platform models

## Engagement Details [.text-h4]

- **Duration:** 1–2 weeks
- **Format:** Remote; management interviews, codebase review (NDA-protected), architecture documentation review, and optional Q&A call with investment committee
- **Deliverable:** Technical due diligence report (10–20 pages) with executive summary, findings, risk scorecard, and overall technical assessment rating
- **Fee:** $12,000–$18,000 depending on company complexity and scope
