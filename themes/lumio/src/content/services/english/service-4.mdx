## What This Looks Like in Practice [.text-h4]

An advisory retainer is not a weekly status call. It's a standing relationship with a senior technical resource who knows your business well enough to give useful input when the decisions actually matter.

That might mean reviewing an architecture proposal before your team builds it, pressure-testing a vendor shortlist, thinking through a hiring decision at the principal engineer level, or helping you articulate the technical narrative for a fundraising round.

## Who This Is For [.text-h4]

- **Startups pre-Series B** that need senior technical input but aren't ready to hire a full-time CTO
- **Growth-stage firms** in streaming, media-tech, or Web3 that have a strong engineering team but lack a principal-level architecture voice
- **Non-technical founders** who want a trusted technical partner for investor conversations and board reporting
- **Investors** who want ongoing technical oversight of a portfolio company

## Engagement Details [.text-h4]

- **Structure:** Monthly retainer, rolling 90-day commitment
- **Hours:** ~8–12 hours per month
- **Format:** Async-first with one monthly video sync; additional sessions available at hourly rate
- **Fee:** $3,500–$4,500 per month (effective rate ~$300–$390/hour for senior strategic access)
