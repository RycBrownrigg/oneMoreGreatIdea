## What This Engagement Covers [.text-h4]

OTT platforms accumulate technical debt quietly. A CDN configuration that worked at 10K concurrent streams becomes a liability at 300K. A DRM integration that shipped in 2019 may not meet today's studio requirements. A microservice boundary that made sense at launch now creates coordination drag across six teams.

This engagement is a structured, expert-led assessment of where your platform stands today — and where the real risks are hiding.

## Who This Is For [.text-h4]

This engagement is best suited for:

- **Startups and growth-stage companies** pre-Series B who need to understand what it will take to scale from current load to 10× or 100×.
- **Established operators** who want an independent architectural perspective before a major platform re-platform or vendor consolidation.
- **Investors and acquirers** who want an architecture-level view of what they're buying or funding — not just a security scan.

## Engagement Details [.text-h4]

- **Duration:** 2–3 weeks
- **Format:** Remote; one or two video sessions for stakeholder interviews plus async review of documentation and architecture diagrams
- **Deliverable:** Written architecture assessment report (15–25 pages) with an executive summary, findings, risk scorecard, and prioritized recommendations
- **Fee:** ~$20,000 fixed
